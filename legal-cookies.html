Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how the Machshev website, operated by Machshev Labs LLC, stores information in your browser.

This policy is an outline to be completed and reviewed by counsel before publication.

1. What the site stores today

The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts. It stores one item in your browser's local storage:

NameTypePurposeKept until
machshev-consentLocal storageRemembers the choice you made in the cookie banner.You clear your browser's site data

Antenna Studio and the PCB Toolkit may keep your preferences in local storage. That data stays in your browser.

2. Optional categories

The cookie banner lets you allow or refuse the optional categories below. None of them is in use yet; your choice is recorded so it applies if they are added.

  • Functional: remembers preferences, such as your last-used settings.
  • Analytics: anonymous page views and performance measurements.
  • Third-party: analytics and advertising services run by other companies.

If any of these are added, list each cookie or script, its provider, its purpose and its lifetime here.

3. Changing your choice

Use the Cookie preferences button at the bottom of any page to change your choice at any time.

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@machshev.com.
