Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information Machshev Labs LLC ("Machshev Labs", "we", "our", or "us") collects through the Machshev website and software, and how it is used.

This policy is an outline to be completed and reviewed by counsel before publication.

1. The website and the online tools

The calculators and Antenna Studio on this site run entirely in your browser. The values you enter and the models you build are not sent to us. Files you open in Antenna Studio are read by your browser, and saving a model downloads it to your computer.

The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google's servers when a page loads. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic request information such as IP addresses.
