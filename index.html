See the fields, 2D, 3D, and Radiation.

Fully integrated viewers for surface currents, volumetric fields, and 3D radiation patterns. Export to CSV, PNG, or Touchstone.

Surface currents on a coupled-resonator filter.

Visualize the passband frequency traverse the structure as each resonator excites.

3D radiation pattern.

Slice per-frequency, 2D polar and 3D patterns.

Full 3D fields.

Full field evolution in time, for the entire domain. Understand where resonant energy lives, how it transfers between geometric components, and make informed design decisions based on the actual physics.

A modern editor that saves you time.

Straightforward layer-based 2.5D editor with standard controls from PCB design software like Altium and KiCad. Parametric geometry and scriptable shapes let you define any PCB-like structure with ease. Cloners, constraints, and Mesh Preview further accelerate design.

Stackup and Geometry together.

Stackup, tools, and primitive list on the left, viewport in the middle, properties on the right. A single visual canvas instead of scattered config files.

Built-in RF calculators.

Patch antenna, microstrip, and 9 more closed-form calculators included. Size traces and elements without switching windows.

Sensible defaults for every setting.

Set the frequency range and quality preset, RayRF automatically scales the air box and PML, refines the mesh inside the board, and stops when sim energy rings out. Advanced mode gives full control when you need it.

Auto-meshing with an interactive preview.

Mesh Preview lets you see how the auto-mesher discretized the geometry. Never a black-box, always transparent settings with an option to copy the auto derived settings to advanced mode for granular control.

S-parameters and Smith Chart.

S11/S21/Sxy magnitude, Smith Chart, and markers that track custom criteria. Interpret matching, bandwidth, and impedance behavior in one place.

Enable Live Mode and edits update the results in seconds.

Tune a patch edge and S11, the Smith Chart, and 3D radiation patterns all re-solve and update in realtime.
