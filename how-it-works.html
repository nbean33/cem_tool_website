Free check and full check.

The free check is fast and catches most layout mistakes. The full check is slower and answers the question that matters: will it fail?

Free check

Flags possible problems.

seconds · no charge
  • Finds the high-speed connections on the board: USB, Ethernet, HDMI, antenna feeds and other controlled-impedance nets.
  • Checks the impedance of every trace segment against the target for that interface, using your manufacturer's stackup.
  • Checks the vias and layer changes along each connection.
  • Checks the length mismatch between the two traces of each differential pair.
  • Marks each connection green, yellow or red, and shows where on the board.

What it does not cover: whether a flagged problem will actually make the connection fail. It checks each feature against a target; it does not simulate the whole path, so losses over frequency, combined reflections, the connector and the chip package are left to the full check.

Full check

Confirms whether it will fail.

minutes · priced per board
  • A detailed electromagnetic simulation of each flagged connection, from driver to receiver, with its vias and connectors.
  • The result compared with what the interface needs to work, for example the eye opening for USB and HDMI or the match at an antenna.
  • A pass or fail for each connection, with the exact spot on the board and the reason.
  • Raw data to download: S-parameters (Touchstone) and the plots as CSV.

Before you run it: the price and the expected run time are shown for the connections you select.
