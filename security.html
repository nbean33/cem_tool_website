Your design stays private.

A board file is your product. Here is exactly what happens to it after you upload it.

Encrypted in transit and at rest.

Files travel to us over an encrypted connection and are stored encrypted. Encryption standard and key management.

Deleted after a set period.

Board files and results are deleted automatically after number of days. You can delete a board and its results yourself at any time from your account, and it is gone straight away.

Never reused.

Your files are used only to run the checks you ask for. They are not shared, not sold, and not used to train models or to improve the product.

Seen by the checks, not by people.

The checks run automatically. Who on the team can access files, and when: for example only during a review call you book, with your permission.

Files that cannot leave your network?

On the Enterprise plan you can self-host: the checks run on your own servers or private cloud, and no board file is ever sent to us.
