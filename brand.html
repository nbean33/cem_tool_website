Brand kit.

The logo, colours and type, and the rules for using them. The look borrows from lab instruments and layout tools: neutral, high contrast, one accent.

Logo

Machshev Labs

The mark is a copper trace between two vias: the thing the product checks, drawn as line art. The trace is the accent blue; the vias are the text colour.

The wordmark is IBM Plex Sans SemiBold. Keep clear space around the logo of at least the height of a via, and do not recolour the mark with the pass, warn or fail colours.

Voice

  • Short, concrete sentences with real numbers: "results in about 4 minutes".
  • Plain words. Engineering terms only where the reader expects them.
  • No buzzwords: not "AI-powered", "revolutionary" or "next-gen".
  • Honest about limits. Say what a check does not cover.
